Privacy by design
The page processes imported files inside this browser only. It has no login service or live synchronisation. Imported records disappear when the page is refreshed or closed; use a protected device and export only the summaries you need.

How to use this with teachers and classes

PRACTICAL WORKFLOW
Before the lesson

Students use their own browser profile and continue their week. For shared tablets, require each student to export at the end of a session and clear or switch the learner profile before the next student.

During the lesson

Use course completion as a prompt, not a mastery grade. Review the saved Arabic words and explanations, then orally check one or two decisions with a fresh phrase.

Between lessons

Import each learner’s next export. Compare the latest submission with previous snapshots and use the gaps to decide who needs revision, source-book reteaching or a transfer task.

This is a local, file-based teacher dashboard—not a secure account system or real-time classroom platform. Do not use it on an unmanaged public computer with identifiable student records. A future shared, multi-teacher live service would need authenticated accounts, access controls, a secure database, retention rules and appropriate parental/Madrassa permissions.
